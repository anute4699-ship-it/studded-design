## What is stored in your browser

We use your browser's local storage, only for things the app needs to work or that you chose:

| Key | What it holds | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `ss:consent` | Your cookie and external-content choice | Essential |
| `ss:age` | That you confirmed you are {{minAge}}+ and accepted the terms, with the date | Essential |
| `ssd:tab` | Which tab of Master Docs you had open | Functional (convenience) |

This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

## Optional external content

| Service | What it does | Data it receives | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Fonts | Loads the display fonts | Your IP address and browser details | **Off.** It is only loaded if you choose **Allow external fonts**. |

If you say no, the app uses fonts already on your device and works exactly the same.
