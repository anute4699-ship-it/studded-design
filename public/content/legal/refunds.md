We want you to be happy with what you paid for. Here is exactly how refunds work. There is no fine print.

## One-off model orders

| Situation | What you get |
|---|---|
| You cancel **before we start** building | A full refund |
| The finished model **doesn't match your brief and references** | A free fix. If we can't fix it, a full refund. |
| You change your mind **after delivery** and the files match the brief | No refund, because you already have the files |

## Monthly plan

- **Cancel at any time.** It's one step, and you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

- After that, we don't refund part-months, but cancelling stops all future charges straight away.

Email **{{supportEmail}}** with the email you paid with and your order or receipt number. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through {{paymentProvider}}. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show them.

This policy is in addition to any rights you have under consumer law where you live, such as the EU/UK 14-day withdrawal right. It does not replace them.
