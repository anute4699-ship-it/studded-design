# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** {{effectiveDate}}

This policy explains what {{productName}} collects, why, who it goes to, and how to delete it. {{productName}} is run by **{{legalName}}**, {{address}}. You can reach us about privacy at **{{privacyEmail}}**.

## What we collect, and nothing more

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The model brief you fill in: name, type, size, notes, palette, part table | When you save a brief | To build the model you asked for |
| Reference images and videos you upload | When you save a brief | To build the model you asked for. **We remove location and camera data (EXIF) from images in your browser before upload.** |
| Game ideas you type into Venture Scout, and your shortlist | When you run an audit or pin an idea | To give you the audit and remember your shortlist |
| Your age confirmation, terms acceptance and cookie choice | On first visit | To follow the law and remember your choices. Stored only in your own browser. |
| Payment details | At checkout | These are handled by {{paymentProvider}}. **We never see or store your card number.** |

We do **not** ask for your real name, phone number, date of birth, location or contacts. We do not run analytics, ad trackers or tracking pixels, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

## How long we keep it

We keep briefs and uploads until you delete them, or until 12 months after your last visit, whichever comes first.

## Your rights, including deletion

- **Delete everything yourself, instantly.** Go to [My data](/my-data) and choose **Delete all my data**. You can also delete single briefs from **Saved briefs**.
- **Ask us.** Email **{{privacyEmail}}** to access, correct, export or delete your data. We answer within {{deletionDays}} days.
- Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or in California), you may have more rights, including the right to complain to your data-protection authority. We honour them for everyone.

## Children

{{productName}} is for people aged **{{minAge}} and over**. We do not knowingly collect data from children under {{minAge}}. The first-visit check blocks anyone who says they are younger. If you are a parent and think your child gave us data, email **{{privacyEmail}}** and we will delete it.

## Emails

We only send emails you need, such as receipts. Your payment provider may send these. We do not send marketing emails. If we ever do, it will only be after you opt in, and every marketing email will have a one-click **unsubscribe** link.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will show a notice in the app before it applies.
